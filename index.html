Provenance

As assets move onchain, there needs to be a credible way to show provenance.

ENS gives LSEG a credible name for each tokenized asset. One name corresponds to each asset, across any blockchain network.

Issuance

Creating an official onchain registry is as easy as a .csv upload.

Add assets with their identifiers and other essential records from a .csv file. Select an asset to open its profile.

Columns: name, ticker, ens (optional), isin, lei, dti, ethereum, solana, stellar. Imports stay in this browser; nothing is written onchain and no ENS or vLEI verification happens.

Compliance

Each asset gets its own profile across chains.

Token addresses on Ethereum, Solana and Stellar sit next to ISIN, LEI, DTI and issuer details, with room for whatever a jurisdiction requires.
